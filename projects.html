A Secure Document Secret Sharing System

using SHA-256-based Share Authentication

Role Undergraduate researcher, led implementation
Team 3, faculty-guided
Timeline 6 months
100%reconstruction accuracy
<6%share-size overhead
<5sreconstruction, files up to 5 MB

The problem

Sensitive documents are risky when one person or one server holds them. Threshold secret sharing splits a file into n shares so that any k of them can rebuild it, and fewer than k reveal nothing. Plain schemes have a gap, though: a tampered share can quietly corrupt the reconstructed document.

What I built

  • A document-level secret-sharing system built on Shamir's (k, n) scheme, tested with (3,5), (4,6) and (5,8) thresholds
  • SHA-256 authentication on every share, so tampered or corrupted shares are rejected before reconstruction
  • A Tkinter desktop interface for splitting, verifying and reconstructing documents

Outcome

Published in the Grenze International Journal of Engineering and Technology (Scopus-indexed, January 2026) and presented at the Hinweis 4th International Conference, ASIT.

Dr. Ratnesh Chaturvedi, Muskaan Jaggi, Snigdha Mishra & Tanvi Khanvilkar

How it works: any three hash-verified shares rebuild the document.

India's CPI Inflation Study

Team 3
Data Government of India, MoSPI
91,558rows of official CPI data

The question

How has consumer inflation moved across India, and are the differences we see between groups and periods statistically real or just noise?

What I did

  • Collected and cleaned 91,558 rows of Consumer Price Index data from the Ministry of Statistics (MoSPI)
  • Ran t-tests, Z-tests, and one-way and two-way ANOVA, with Tukey HSD post-hoc tests to find which groups actually differ
  • Built OLS, Ridge and Lasso regression models with cross-validation to project CPI for 2026
  • Deployed everything as an interactive Streamlit dashboard

The idea

Election results and candidate disclosures are public, but they sit in separate places and are hard to read together. I wanted one database and one dashboard where anyone could explore both.

What I built

  • A relational database over certified ECI results and ADR candidate affidavit data
  • A FastAPI backend with authentication and a JavaScript dashboard on top
  • Views for party-wise seats and vote share, state turnout, the closest and safest seats by margin, and search by constituency
  • Transparency analytics: women's representation, declared criminal cases, education and assets, broken down by party
